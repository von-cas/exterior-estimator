Exterior Estimator: how it is built

A HOVER measurement report goes in. A customer estimate and a production material list for roofing, siding, windows and gutters come out. One rule decides what every part is allowed to do.

Build order

  1. Discoveryabout 1 week10 to 20 past jobs with report, estimate and materials actually ordered. This becomes the test set.
  2. MVPabout 4 weeksRoofing and gutters end to end: upload, review, estimate, takeoff.
  3. All four tradesabout 4 weeksSiding and windows, templates, price history, roles. Runs beside the current method.
  4. Connected4 to 6 weeksHOVER API, CRM, QuickBooks, purchase orders by email, then by supplier API.
  5. ScaleongoingMore trades, actual vs estimated material feedback, reporting.

Accuracy is tested the same way at every stage: each past job is re-run on every change, and a result outside the agreed tolerance blocks the release.

Durations are estimates for one engineer and will be confirmed after discovery. Material prices are fictional.
