Exterior Estimator: how it is built

A HOVER measurement report goes in. A customer estimate and a production material list for roofing, siding, windows and gutters come out. One rule decides what every part is allowed to do.

AI readsIt copies the numbers off the report, with the page each one came from.It never does math and never fills in a missing value.
Code calculatesIt checks every number, then works out quantities, cost and price with tested formulas.Same input, same answer, every time.
People approveThey clear the flags and answer what no report can know.Measurements, the estimate and each purchase order need a person's OK.
Sample: HOVER Complete Measurements, 27 pages Roof 2,318 sq ft at 5/12 Siding 2,513 sq ft 34 windows Eaves 176 ft 8 in
The parts

Five parts, and how they talk

Say it in one sentence

One web app does the work. The AI, the database and n8n each have one narrow job around it.

  1. Staff sign in and use the web app in a browser. Each role sees only what it should.
  2. A HOVER report arrives as an uploaded PDF, or as data straight from HOVER's API.
  3. For a PDF, the AI reads it and returns named numbers with their pages. With HOVER's data there is nothing to read, so no AI is used.
  4. Everything is saved in one database: catalog, prices, estimates, logins and the report files.
  5. n8n collects finished events and passes them to the CRM, accounting and suppliers. It never calculates.

The data

A product or price change never means rebuilding the estimate

Say it in one sentence

The estimate stores the inputs, never the answer. Change a product, the waste or a price, and the line recalculates.

The plan

Build order

Say it in one sentence

Past jobs become the test set. Roofing and gutters ship first, then the other trades, then the connections.

  1. Discoveryabout 1 week10 to 20 past jobs with report, estimate and materials actually ordered. This becomes the test set.
  2. MVPabout 4 weeksRoofing and gutters end to end: upload, review, estimate, takeoff.
  3. All four tradesabout 4 weeksSiding and windows, templates, price history, roles. Runs beside the current method.
  4. Connected4 to 6 weeksHOVER API, CRM, QuickBooks, purchase orders by email, then by supplier API.
  5. ScaleongoingMore trades, actual vs estimated material feedback, reporting.

Accuracy is tested the same way at every stage: each past job is re-run on every change, and a result outside the agreed tolerance blocks the release.

Durations are estimates for one engineer and will be confirmed after discovery. Material prices are fictional.
